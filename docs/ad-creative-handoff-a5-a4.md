## 4. Art direction

- **Feel:** premium heritage, not rodeo merch, not tacky.
- **Palette:** warm cream, charcoal ink, saddle tan, deep gingham red, sage/khaki, sun-faded denim.
- **Type:** Fraunces (headlines), Archivo (utility text), a stamp keyline. Token values to be supplied by the brand file (no hex codes are recorded in this brief).
- **Photography:** real kids and families outdoors, natural light, rural cues (paddock, timber, fence). No studio flat-lays. Avoid the corrugated-iron wall used in the current A4.
- **Voice:** warm, story-driven, family-first. No hard-sell language.
- **Wording rule (owner, 21 Oct):** never write "made" in new copy or on-image text; always say "designed". So "Made by a country mum" becomes "Designed by a country mum", "So I made them" becomes "So I designed them", "Made to fit real life" becomes "Designed to fit real life", and the "family-made" badge becomes **"family-designed"**. This applies to new ads only; live ads (for example R3) are not edited.
- **Badges (consistent use):** **reviews**, **best seller**, **Aussie designed** and **family-designed** are the working set (all confirmed by the owner). **Best seller** is live and confirmed (owner, 21 Oct). "Bundle and save" is not live, so it stays out of all copy and badges. Each badge must be true for every product it appears on.
- **Imagery of children:** use the owner's existing Shopify photos only. No generated or synthetic images of children. Where an existing photo is only a stand-in, label it "PLACEHOLDER" in the file name.
